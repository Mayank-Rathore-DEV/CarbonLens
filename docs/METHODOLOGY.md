
# CarbonLens — Methodology

## 1. Purpose

CarbonLens estimates greenhouse gas emissions
associated with selected personal lifestyle activities.

## 2. Initial Categories

The initial version covers:

- Household electricity
- LPG consumption
- Personal transportation
- Food consumption

## 3. Excluded Categories

These categories are not included in the initial calculation:

- Shopping
- Waste
- Water consumption
- Digital services
- Carbon offsets

They may be added in future versions after suitable
scientific data and calculation methods are established.

## 4. Calculation Formula

Emissions = Activity Data × Emission Factor

Results are expressed in kg CO2e.

## 5. Data Sources

CarbonLens aims to prioritize:

- Official Indian government datasets
- Peer-reviewed scientific research
- International scientific publications where Indian data is unavailable

## 6. Scientific Principles

Every emission factor should have:

- A traceable source
- A defined unit
- A relevant geographical scope
- A data year
- Documented limitations

## 7. Limitations

Carbon footprint results are estimates, not direct measurements.

Results depend on the quality of user-provided data,
emission factors, and calculation assumptions.

## 8. Future Improvements

- Regional emission factors
- Better food-related datasets
- Uncertainty estimates
- Independent scientific review

## 9. Electricity Reporting Period

CarbonLens will initially use Indian financial years
for annual electricity-emission calculations.

Each electricity consumption period must be matched
with the corresponding CEA emission factor.

The application will not automatically use the latest
factor for historical electricity consumption.

The initial electricity factor is expressed in kg CO2/kWh,
not kg CO2e/kWh.

The selected factor and its source version must be
preserved with every calculation.
