
# CarbonLens 🌱

**An India-first Personal Carbon Footprint Platform**

## About the Project

CarbonLens is a project focused on helping individuals understand their personal carbon footprint and the environmental impact of their everyday activities.

The goal is to make carbon footprint calculation simple, transparent, and scientifically informed.

## What CarbonLens Aims to Do

- Calculate personal carbon footprints.
- Estimate emissions from electricity consumption.
- Estimate emissions from transportation.
- Understand emissions associated with food consumption.
- Provide practical suggestions to reduce environmental impact.
- Save footprint estimates locally and compare successive calculations.
- Show category-level history and transparent change comparisons.
- Help users track their progress over time.

## Initial Focus

The first version focuses on four categories:

1. Household Electricity
2. LPG Consumption
3. Personal Transportation
4. Food Consumption

## Our Scientific Approach

CarbonLens follows an activity-data and emission-factor approach.

**Emissions = Activity Data × Emission Factor**

Every emission factor should have a documented source, unit, geographical relevance, and data year.

## Mobile App Status

The Expo mobile app includes a multi-step calculator, local saved-estimate history, a dashboard connected to the latest saved result, and comparisons between successive estimates. History is stored on-device with AsyncStorage; it is not synced to a cloud account. The comparison is between saved calculations, not a measured monthly trend.

## Project Status

CarbonLens is evolving from an initial web prototype into a more comprehensive carbon footprint platform.

## Long-Term Vision

To build an accessible, transparent, and useful carbon footprint platform for individuals across India.
